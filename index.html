Shore Power Gap Atlas

Where ships emit at berth, and where shore power is missing. AFIR readiness of 31 EU ports set against at-berth CO₂ inventories for Dutch, Norwegian and Finnish ports.

Map

Squares show AFIR readiness as DNV rated it in 2024: whether each port has enough shore-power connection points installed or contracted for its container, cruise and passenger ships above 5,000 GT. Circles show at-berth CO₂, sized by tonnes per year and coloured by the share that the 2030 obligation covers.

Squares, AFIR readiness (DNV rating): no segment met (21 ports) some segments met (7) all segments met (none in 2024) not concerned by AFIR (3)
Circles, at-berth CO₂ (size = kt per year): half or more inside the 2030 obligation mostly outside it

The Dutch case

The Netherlands is the one country where both layers meet: the national Emissieregistratie publishes at-berth CO₂ by municipality and ship type, and DNV assessed four Dutch ports (Terneuzen is outside the AFIR maritime mandate). Two different gaps appear.

Installed or contracted connection points against the number DNV estimates each port needs to serve 90% of 2023 traffic. Only signed contracts count. Click a column to sort.

Method and sources

Supply

DNV (2024), Status for onshore power supply in selected EU ports, report 2024-1792 for Transport & Environment, Table 4-1. Data collected through port interviews in March and April 2024; required points estimated from 2023 AIS traffic. A surplus in one segment is not counted against a shortfall in another. Venice's passenger requirement is shown but left out of the totals, as DNV flags it as doubtful. Source

At-berth CO₂, Netherlands

Emissieregistratie, dataset 1990-2024 (definitive), source "Zeescheepvaart stilliggend", compartment air, by municipality and ship type. Port areas group municipalities (for example Rotterdam with Schiedam, Vlaardingen and Maassluis). The national figures include auxiliary boilers and all ship sizes. Source

At-berth CO₂, Norway and Finland

DecarbPort inventory from open port-call registers (Kystverket Kystdatahuset; Fintraffic Digitraffic, CC BY 4.0), auxiliary engines only, method of Cunha et al. (2026). The 2030 share applies the obligation to container and passenger ships above 5,000 GT staying more than two hours, in ports above the AFIR call thresholds. Nordic At-Berth CO₂

Limits

  • The supply layer is a 2024 snapshot of 31 ports, not every TEN-T port.
  • The two CO₂ sources use different methods and years; compare shapes, not decimals.
  • Port positions are approximate centroids.

Context

The EEA reports that 71 of 179 TEN-T core ports in the EU had shore-side electricity (461 berths) and 31 of 371 comprehensive ports (78 berths), without a downloadable dataset. EEA (2025)
